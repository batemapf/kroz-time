01 THE REAL THING

This is Scott Miller’s original DOS game running in your browser, including the “BIG TROUBLE” map in level 4. The 80 × 25 character display, 16-color palette, PC-speaker effects, and real-time creatures all come from the game itself.

02 KNOW YOUR KEYS

Arrow keys move; Home, End, Page Up and Page Down move diagonally. W cracks your whip, T teleports, P pauses, and I opens instructions from the original main menu. This is not turn-based. The dungeon won’t wait for you.

03 SAVE YOUR EXPEDITION

Press S in the game and follow the prompts to save to slot A, B, or C. Press R to restore. Saves persist in this browser when local storage is available. Original saves restart the current level. Reboot keeps your saved slots.
